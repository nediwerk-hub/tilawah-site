Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

What stays on your phone

Where you were reading, the pages you read, your streak, saved verses, plans, downloads, recordings and settings stay on this device. We cannot see them. Delete the app and they are gone; Profile → Recited → Delete my recitation data removes recordings and history while you keep the app.

Your voice

When you recite, the microphone audio is handled on the device by the speech model inside the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you turn on Keep my audio, the recordings are stored on this device only.

Your account, if you make one

Tilawah works without an account. If you create one, we store your email address, your name, your role and the date you joined on our server at Supabase in Frankfurt (EU); your password is stored there in protected form. Profile → My account → Delete account removes all of it at once.

What the app downloads

Recitations are streamed or downloaded from quran.com and everyayah.com, and the speech model from GitHub. Like any website you visit, those services see your internet address. Nothing about you is sent along with the request.

Feedback

Send us feedback sends your message, together with the app version, iOS version, device model and language, to our server, which emails it to us. Your email address is included only if you type it.

No tracking

No adverts, no analytics, no trackers, no data brokers. We never sell data, and your recitations are not used to train anything.

Children

Tilawah is safe for children. There are no public profiles, no chat and nothing to buy.

Questions

Use Send us feedback in the app (Profile → About Tilawah) and a person will answer.
