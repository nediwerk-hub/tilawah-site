Using Tilawah

Reading the mushaf, listening and reciting are free, with no daily limit. The app works without an account; if you make one, it is for one person.

The Quran text

The Uthmani text follows the King Fahd Complex (Hafs) edition. We do not change a letter of it. Feedback from the app is a study aid, not a fatwa — for tajweed rulings, ask a qualified teacher.

Recitation feedback

Following your recitation is an early version. It can miss a word or mark one wrongly, and it needs a quiet room and a clear voice.

Nothing to pay

Tilawah is free and has nothing to buy. The extra themes and the theme builder are earned by reciting, so there is no subscription, no payment and nothing that can expire.

What you may not do

Do not resell access, scrape the app, or record anyone who has not agreed to it.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that affects you, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
